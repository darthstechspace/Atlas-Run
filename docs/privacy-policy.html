Atlas Run Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026. App version: 0.5.0-beta

Atlas Run (“Atlas Run,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a location-based exploration game for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Atlas Run, you agree to this policy.

1. Who operates the app

Atlas Run is published by the developer listed on the Apple App Store and/or Google Play Store listing for the app (“Operator”). For privacy requests, use the support or contact email shown on that store listing, or the contact section at the end of this policy.

2. Summary

  • Core gameplay requires location on your device (GPS).
  • You can play offline without an account; progress stays on your phone.
  • Cloud sync is optional and uses your Operator’s Supabase project when you sign in.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Safe Mode obfuscates location before it is uploaded to the cloud.
  • Other players do not receive your exact GPS or run routes through social features.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Location (required for gameplay)

With your permission, Atlas Run accesses device location services to:

  • Show your position on the map and move the map as you walk
  • Discover nearby landmarks, treasure chests, and fog-of-war exploration
  • Measure distance during active runs and ghost runs
  • Evaluate play-safety alerts (e.g., unusually high speed) when that toggle is enabled

While using the app: foreground location is used continuously on the Map tab and during runs.

During runs (optional background): if you grant “Always” / “Allow all the time” location permission, the app may continue receiving location updates while the screen is off so run distance is recorded. You can deny background permission and keep the app open instead.

On your device: your game save stores progress locally (including areas explored and landmarks found).

In the cloud (only if signed in and syncing): your profile may store last_lat and last_lng to restore map position. If Safe Mode is enabled in Profile, those coordinates are rounded to an approximate grid (about 500 meters) with a user-specific offset before upload. Exact GPS used for gameplay on the device is not uploaded when Safe Mode is on.

3.2 Photos and camera (optional)

If you set a profile picture, you may choose an image from your photo library or take a photo with the camera. The image is stored in your local game save. If cloud sync is enabled, it may be uploaded as your avatar URL in your Supabase profile.

3.3 Account and game data (optional)

If you create an account (email/password or OAuth where enabled), we may store:

  • Email address and authentication identifiers (via Supabase Auth)
  • Username and display fields you choose
  • Game progress: level, XP, gems, milestones, cosmetics, equipped items, quests, badges, titles
  • Collected landmarks, unlocked map areas, run history uploaded for leaderboards
  • Friend relationships and friend requests (pending/accepted)
  • Weekly stats used for leaderboards (e.g., distance, landmarks found, fog cleared, level)

Social features show other users your username, optional social banner, and aggregate stats, not your live location or GPS trail.

3.4 Local device storage

  • AsyncStorage: offline game save (progress, settings, map theme preference)
  • SecureStore: encrypted storage for sign-in session tokens when you use an account
  • Onboarding flags: whether you completed the in-app tutorial

3.5 Technical and diagnostic data

The app may log limited technical messages in development builds. Production builds do not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs in this codebase. Store builds are produced with Expo Application Services (EAS), which processes build metadata under Expo’s policies.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and improve gameplay (map, landmarks, runs, shop, quests)
  • Save and restore your progress locally and, if you opt in, in the cloud
  • Enable friends, leaderboards, and cloud backup when Supabase is configured
  • Display your profile and optional avatar to you and your friends
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we rely on:

  • Contract / service: processing needed to run the game you requested
  • Consent: location, photos, camera, and optional background location (you can withdraw in device Settings)
  • Legitimate interests: securing the service, preventing abuse, and improving stability

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only as follows:

  • Service providers you or the Operator configure (see Section 7)
  • Other players: limited profile and leaderboard fields per friend/social settings; not precise location
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security
  • Business transfers: if the app or its assets are transferred, subject to this policy

7. Third-party services

Depending on how the Operator configures the app:

  • Google Maps: map tiles and styling when a Google Maps API key is set in the build. Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: authentication, database, and file storage for accounts and cloud sync. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS: mobile app build infrastructure for developers. Expo Privacy Policy

Offline play without Supabase keeps gameplay data on your device only (except map tiles from Google if configured).

8. Your choices and controls

  • Permissions: revoke Location, Camera, or Photos in iOS Settings or Android Settings
  • Safe Mode: Profile → approximate location only when syncing to the cloud
  • Play safety alerts: Profile → toggle speed warnings
  • Offline play: do not sign in; no cloud upload occurs
  • Sign out: Profile → removes local session; cloud data remains until deleted
  • Reset local save: Profile → clears on-device progress
  • Delete cloud account: contact the Operator or use Supabase account deletion if enabled on the project

9. Data retention

  • Local save: until you uninstall the app or use Reset local save
  • Cloud data: until you delete your account or the Operator deletes the Supabase project data
  • Leaderboard / friend data: retained while your account exists unless removed by the Operator

10. Security

We use industry-standard practices available through Supabase (HTTPS, access controls) and on-device encrypted storage for session tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Children’s privacy

Atlas Run is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child provided data and we will delete it.

12. International users

If you use the app outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using the app, you understand your data may be transferred to jurisdictions with different privacy laws.

13. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may have rights to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. Atlas Run does not sell personal information as defined under California law. To exercise rights, contact the Operator using the email on the store listing.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. Material changes may also be noted in app release notes.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact the Operator at the support email address listed on the App Store or Google Play page for Atlas Run.

When contacting us, include the email associated with your account (if any) and the platform you use (iOS or Android).
